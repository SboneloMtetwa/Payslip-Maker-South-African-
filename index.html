How this works: PAYE is calculated on the 2026/27 SARS individual tax tables (year of assessment 1 Mar 2026 – 28 Feb 2027), applying the primary/secondary/tertiary rebates and, where selected, the s6A medical scheme fees credit. UIF is 1% of remuneration for the employee (and 1% employer), capped at a monthly earnings ceiling of R17,712 (max R177.12/month/side). Provident fund contributions reduce taxable income up to the s11F cap (27.5% of remuneration, max R430,000/year). When you enter a net salary, the tool solves for the gross salary that produces that exact take-home pay. Aksiyona eyo lamthuthu.
